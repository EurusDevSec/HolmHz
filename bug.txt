## Benchmark Results Summary

### Overall Metrics (OOD = primary fair metric)

| Model | Params | ID AUC | OOD AUC | OOD Acc | OOD F1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HolmHz v4** | 4M | **0.9959** | **0.7823** | **71.3%** | **0.7547** |
| CNNDetection | 25M | 0.5882 | 0.4264 | 44.0% | 0.0052 |
| UniversalFakeDetect | 300M | 0.6479 | 0.4674 | 44.1% | 0.0306 |
| DeepfakeBench | 19M | 0.5237 | 0.3913 | 42.8% | 0.4203 |

### Per-Source OOD Accuracy

| Model | flux | tristanzhang_fake | real_pexels | real_camera |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HolmHz** | **77.5%** | **79.3%** | **74.5%** | 36.0% |
| CNNDetection | 0.0% | 0.3% | 99.0% | 100.0% |
| UniversalFakeDetect | 0.0% | 2.0% | 98.0% | 98.0% |
| DeepfakeBench | 57.5% | 31.7% | 47.5% | 55.0% |

### Key Findings

1. **HolmHz WINS on OOD** — AUC 0.7823 vs next best 0.4674 (UniversalFakeDetect). Nearly 2x better.

2. **SOTA models completely fail on Diffusion fakes:**
   - CNNDetection: **0.0% on flux, 0.3% on tristanzhang** — classifies ALL diffusion fakes as real
   - UniversalFakeDetect: **0.0% on flux, 2.0% on tristanzhang** — same failure pattern
   - DeepfakeBench: 57.5% on flux (barely above random) — best SOTA but still poor

3. **SOTA models overpredict "real"** — CNNDetection (100%/99%) and UniversalFakeDetect (98%/98%) get near-perfect accuracy on real images but can't detect ANY modern fakes. They essentially always predict "real".

4. **Training data diversity > model size:**
   - HolmHz: **4M params, 48.5MB** trained on GAN+Diffusion data → OOD AUC 0.78
   - CLIP-UFD: **300M params, 900MB** trained on ProGAN only → OOD AUC 0.47
   - 75x fewer params, **1.67x better OOD AUC**

5. **HolmHz weakness: `real_camera` (36.0%)** — known limitation from v4. SOTA models don't help here either (their high accuracy on reals is because they predict everything as real).
